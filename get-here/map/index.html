Get here

CEICO is part of the Institute of Physics of the Czech Academy of Sciences (FZU) in Prague 8 – Libeň, a short walk from Ládví metro station. Google Maps and Mapy.com know the public transport schedules and can find you the quickest route.

Main entrance

Pod Vodárenskou věží 1
182 00 Praha 8

Route in Google Maps (opens in a new tab) Route in Mapy.com (opens in a new tab)

Enter the door shown in the photo and announce yourself to the doorman, asking for your host. We are on the 2nd floor of the FZU, mostly to the right of the staircase. Office numbers are on the Team pages.

Postal address: Na Slovance 1999/2, 182 21 Praha 8

Directions

  1. From the airport by public transport

    about 1 hourTrolleybus 59Metro AMetro C

    1. At the airport, buy a 90-minute public transport ticket (40 CZK) in a booth to the left after exiting from baggage claim.
    2. Take trolleybus no. 59 from outside the terminal to Nádraží Veleslavín.
    3. Change to metro line A (green), direction Depo Hostivař. Exit at Muzeum.
    4. Change to metro line C (red), direction Letňany. Exit at Ládví.
    5. Follow the walking directions from Ládví.

  2. From Prague main train station

    about 25 minutesMetro C

    1. Take metro line C (red), direction Letňany. Exit at Ládví.
    2. Follow the walking directions from Ládví.

  3. Walking from Ládví metro station

    about 10 minutes

    1. Exit Ládví station through the underground passage with murals of pandas on the wall, under Střelničná street. Then turn left.
    2. Turn right on Davídkova street and right again on Pod Vodárenskou věží, once you pass the old tower on your left. The institute will be on your right.
    3. Some shortcuts are possible, but it is easy to get lost; the map shows the way.

  4. From the airport by taxi

    about 30 minutes

    1. Taxis in Prague have a bad reputation for overcharging tourists. If you take one, make sure that the displayed fare is the fixed CZK 28 per km set by the city and that the driver turns on the meter. The total fare should be around CZK 700. You do not need to tip.
    2. Uber operates in Prague, picking up passengers from the arrivals floor of the airport terminal (upstairs, instead of departures). The fare should be around CZK 400.
    3. Give Pod Vodárenskou věží 1, Praha 8 as the address.
