I am interested in various topics in String theory. The area which most interests me at the moment is the Ambitwistor String theory and its applications to AdS/CFT. My current research is looking at vertex operators in AdS backgrounds. I have conducted some research in the past on Ambitwistor string theory, in particular an investigation into the structure of the BRST charge of an Ambitwistor String theory when represented in spinorial variables as it leads to a reducible guage theory.
