About CEICO

The Central European Institute for Cosmology and Fundamental Physics (CEICO) is a centre within the Institute of Physics of the Czech Academy of Sciences. We are an international research group focused on the interplay between cosmology, gravity and string theory.

CEICO was created in November 2016 with the financial support of the European Structural and Investment Funds, Czech state funds of the Ministry of Education, Youth and Sports, the European Research Council and the Czech Science Foundation.

Research

All research →

  • Cosmology

    Fundamental problems posed by observations of our Universe, from dark energy and modified gravity to inflation and its alternatives.

  • Gravity

    The oldest known force, yet the least understood. We work towards a more complete description of gravity on microscopic and cosmological scales.

  • String theory

    Foundations of string theory, applications of two-dimensional conformal field theory, and holography for higher-spin gravity in three dimensions.

  • Instrumentation for cosmology

    Developing and testing sensors for cosmology surveys, including CCDs for the Rubin Observatory LSST camera and next-generation CMOS sensors.

One-loop self-energy of Type II heavy string states

Lorenzo Grimaldi Tor Vergata University of Rome, Italy

Abstract

The free string spectrum is highly degenerate, with a degeneracy that grows exponentially with the mass, making highly excited strings natural candidates for black-hole microstates in the string/black-hole correspondence. A non-vanishing string coupling $g_s$ makes massive states unstable and shifts their masses, partially lifting this degeneracy. At one loop, the imaginary part of the self-energy gives the tree-level width for the decay into two lower-mass states, whereas the real part gives the mass shift and is generally IR-divergent. We firstly focus on the states of maximal spin at each level in the NS-NS sector of Type II theories, for which the analysis simplifies because they cannot mix with other states. Exploiting elliptic functions, we obtain a closed-form expression for the worldsheet integral on the torus. We then regularize and renormalize the remaining modular integral via the $i\varepsilon$-prescription, extracting mass shifts and decay widths up to level $N=10$.

Towards the explicit construction of generic 2d CFTs

António Antunes LPENS, Paris, France

Abstract

Our understanding of interacting CFTs in d>2 dimensions was revolutionized by modern developments in the analytical and numerical conformal bootstrap. The poster child of this program, the non-exactly solvable 3d Ising CFT, is now understood to an unprecedented level of detail. In contrast, our understanding of 2d CFTs encompasses large classes of exactly solvable models (the so-called rational CFTs), and a set of universal bootstrap results which apply only to "generic" non-exactly solvable CFTs of which no concrete example is available. In this talk, we summarize our recent attempts at constructing such theories by coupling exactly solvable CFTs and flowing to IR fixed points. We will discuss perturbative and non-perturbative constructions as well as some dual bounds on the set of conserved currents in the IR..
