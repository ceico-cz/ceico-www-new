Funding

CEICO is funded by a combination of grants awarded by European agencies and national Czech funding bodies.

  1. European Structural and Investment Funds · Ministry of Education, Youth and Sports

    Cosmology, Gravity and the Dark Sector

    The project to create CEICO was selected in 2016 in a competition organised by the Ministry of Education, Youth and Sports of the Czech Republic. It is supported by a combination of funds from the European Structural and Investment Funds and state funds of the Czech Republic.

    Grant number
    CZ.02.1.01/0.0/0.0/15_003/0000437
    Period
    2017–2022

  2. European Research Council · Consolidator Grant

    Theories and Models of the Dark Sector: Dark Matter, Dark Energy and Gravity

    Principal investigator: Constantinos Skordis

    As of 1 September 2016, the Institute of Physics is the primary beneficiary (coordinator) of this grant.

    Awarded
    2014
    Amount
    €1.2 million

  3. Czech Science Foundation (GAČR)

    Two grants in string theory and conformal field theory

    Principal investigator: Martin Schnabl

    The grants support the group's work in string theory, conformal field theory and related areas in mathematics.

  4. Czech Academy of Sciences

    J. E. Purkyně Fellowship

    Principal investigator: Alexander Vikman

    The fellowship supports his research in inflationary models of the early universe.
