Research

CEICO studies the Universe on all scales, from the quantum structure of space and time to the largest structures we observe. Our four groups work closely together and share a weekly seminar programme.

  1. 8 members

    Cosmology

    CEICO's Cosmology group focuses on fundamental problems posed by observations of our Universe.

  2. 9 members

    Gravity

    Gravity is the oldest force known, yet the least understood. The CEICO Gravity group works on developing a more complete description of gravity at a fundamental level both on microscopic and on cosmological scales.

    • +2
    Explore gravity

  3. 8 members

    String theory

    The String Theory group at CEICO focuses on foundations of string theory, applications of 2D conformal field theory, and holography for 3D higher spin gravity.

    Explore string theory

  4. 3 members

    Instrumentation for cosmology

    The instrumentation group works on developing and testing instrumentation, especially CCDs for the camera of the Large Synoptic Survey Telescope. We are also working together with Brookhaven National Laboratory to develop next-generation CMOS sensors for future cosmology surveys.
